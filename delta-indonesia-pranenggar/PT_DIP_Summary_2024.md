# PT. Delta Indonesia Pranenggar (DIP) — Company Profile Summary 2024

## Overview

PT. Delta Indonesia Pranenggar (Delta Indonesia) is a service company operating in the field of **Human Resource Development (HRD)** through formal and non-formal education and training, helping organizations fulfil Occupational Safety and Health (OSH/K3) requirements in accordance with statutory regulations. The company is headquartered in Bekasi and is part of the Delta Indonesia Group.

---

## Company History

| Period | Milestone |
|--------|-----------|
| **Pre-1999** | Established as a legal entity (Foundation/Yayasan) based on Notarial Deed of Mrs. Maria Kristina Soeharyo, SH, domiciled in Jakarta. |
| **Dec 9, 1999** | Foundation built a cooperation with PT. Duta Wisnurama for cruise ship workforce placement. |
| **2000 – 2006** | Operated as an Education & Professional Development Institution. |
| **2006 – Present** | Transformed into an OSH Service Company (PJK3) under the name PT. Delta Indonesia Pranenggar, based on Notarial Deed of Mrs. Tati Nurwati, SH. |

---

## Vision

To be at the forefront of Human Resources (HR) development specializing in:
- Occupational Safety and Health (OSH/K3)
- Productivity
- Management Consulting
- Other skill training services

...by providing the best satisfaction and quality for customers and work partners.

> *"Menjadi yang terdepan dalam pengembangan Sumber Daya Manusia (SDM) di Bidang Keselamatan Kesehatan Kerja (K3), Produktivitas, Konsultan Manajemen dan Jasa Pelatihan Ketrampilan lainnya."*

---

## Missions

1. Provide various HR trainings and consultancies in OSH, Productivity, and other skill training services — based on competence and regulations — to support company performance.
2. Take active roles in HR empowerment activities in OSH, Productivity, and other skill training at national and international scale.
3. Provide various management consulting services for government and private institutions, referring to Indonesian regulations and international standards.
4. Facilitate HR empowerment activities in OSH, Productivity, and skill training needed by government and private institutions.
5. Build cooperation and collaboration with government and private institutions domestically and overseas.

---

## Quality Policy

1. Be a reliable company in HRD, OSH, Productivity, and other skills based on competence and regulations.
2. Improve services for customers and work partners by delivering the best satisfaction and quality.
3. Improve the quality and competence of PT. Delta Indonesia Pranenggar's own HR in service delivery, policy making, planning, and program implementation.
4. Maintain and improve cooperation with private and government institutions related to HR quality improvement.
5. Be consistent in maintaining service quality for customers and work partners.

---

## Quality Objectives

1. Increase the number of annual training participants by at least **10% per year** compared to the previous year.
2. Achieve over **80% customer satisfaction** based on evaluation of instructors, facilitators, and organizers (PIC).
3. Acquire at least **5 new customers** and **3 new work partners** from private or government institutions every year.
