# PT. Biro Sertifikasi Indonesia (BSI) — Company Profile Summary 2024

## Overview

PT. Biro Sertifikasi Indonesia (BSI) is a subsidiary of PT. Delta Indonesia Group. The company operates as an independent, competitive, and reliable **Audit and Management System Certification** services institution at both national and international levels.

---

## Vision

To become an independent, competitive, and trusted Audit and Management System Certification institution, at both national and international level.

> *"Bertekad untuk menjadi Lembaga Jasa Audit dan Sertifikasi di bidang Sistem Manajemen yang Independen, Kompetitif serta Terpercaya, baik di tingkat nasional maupun Internasional."*

---

## Missions

1. Build partnerships with stakeholders to find the best solutions for ensuring requirements fulfilment and performance improvement.
2. Develop competent Human Resources in accordance with company values.
3. Provide the best services and added value for customers in conducting audit and management system certification.

## Products & Services

### 1. SMK3, SIMPRO & ISO Certification
- SMK3 Certification — based on Government Regulation No. 50 of 2012
- SIMPRO Certification — based on Minister Decree No. 156 of 2021
- ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001

### 2. GAP Analysis
Audit simulation to verify the best fulfilment of management systems (SMK3, SIMPRO, or ISO).

### 3. Internal Audit
Assistance with implementing Internal Audit within a company before conducting External Audit.
