# PT. Delta Nusantara Persada (DNP) — Company Profile Summary 2024

## Overview

PT. Delta Nusantara Persada is a company operating in the field of **work equipment inspection & testing (riksa uji), equipment audit services, and building technical studies**, helping clients fulfil regulatory requirements and applicable standards.

---

## Vision

To be at the forefront of providing reliable and trustworthy **Consulting Services** and **Work Equipment Testing & Inspection Services**.

> *"Menjadi yang terdepan dalam Penyediaan Pelayanan Jasa Konsultan dan Jasa Pemeriksaan Pengujian peralatan kerja yang dapat diandalkan serta terpercaya."*

---

## Missions

1. Provide various services in consulting, work equipment testing and inspection to support productivity improvement and occupational safety in both government and private sectors.
2. Provide the best services for all customers and become a reliable and trustworthy work partner.

---

## Quality Policy (Kebijakan Mutu)

1. Ensure the satisfaction of clients and work partners.
2. Conduct sustainable service improvement.
3. Be honest and professional in all works.
4. Be on time in performing all works.
5. Be informative.
6. Obey clients' requirements and applicable legislation.
7. Be astute in seeing opportunities for improvement.
8. Planned education for improving employee competence.

---

## HSE Policy (Kebijakan K3)

1. Obey legislation and other applicable requirements.
2. Improve performance sustainably.
3. Prevent injuries and occupational illnesses that affect the company business process.
4. Provide training and safe, convenient work facilities for all stakeholders.

---

## Products & Services

### 1. Work Equipment Testing & Inspection (Riksa Uji Peralatan)
- Lifting & Transport Equipment
- Electricity Installation & Lightning Rod
- Elevator & Escalator
- Fire Protection Installation
- Boiler & Pressure Vessel
- Production Equipment
- Building Construction

### 2. Equipment Audit Services (Jasa Audit Alat)
Inspection of systems, processes, or products, covering:
- Elevator & Escalator
- Heavy Equipment
- Electricity Installation & Lightning Rod
- Fire Protection
- Etcetera

### 3. Construction Technical Studies (Kajian Teknis Bangunan)
Finding conformity between design and actual conditions, covering:
- Architecture
- Construction
- Weak Current Electricity (LAL)
- Strong Current Electricity (LAK)
- Sanitation, Drainage & Plumbing (SDP)
- Building Air Conditioning (TUG)
- In-Building Transportation (TDG)
